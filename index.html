Distilling-Hate

Manuscript Under Review at PLOS ONE

Undergraduate capstone research on knowledge distillation for efficient Bangla hate-speech classification, compressing BanglaBERT Large into a substantially smaller student model for resource-constrained deployment.

JSSL-GI: Self-Supervised ViT for GI Endoscopy Classification

Undergraduate Research Project

Designed a self-supervised learning pipeline for gastrointestinal endoscopy classification using MAE reconstruction and multi-view InfoNCE with an ImageNet-pretrained ViT-Small backbone.

METHODMAE reconstruction + multi-view InfoNCE
BACKBONEImageNet-pretrained ViT-Small
OPTIMIZATIONAdaptive uncertainty-based loss weighting
RESULTS92.00% accuracy · 67.30% macro-F1 · 96.99% MCC
